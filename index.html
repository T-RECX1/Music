What this prototype does and does not doread before judging results
  • Realistic: trimming, normalizing, pitch detection, browser synth rendering, STFT-style fingerprints, envelope comparison, and a genuine genetic algorithm.
  • Simplified: the browser synth is less powerful than Serum, Vital, Omnisphere, or BandLab instruments. It can approximate simple tones, bells, keys, pads, and plucks, but it will miss complex sampled instruments.
  • Important: matching synth timbre is a search problem, not magic. Better results usually come from cleaner target notes, narrower synth parameter ranges, and more generations.
  • Worth building next: Python rendering with NumPy/SciPy/librosa/pedalboard would make the feature extraction and effects scoring more serious.
